Outbound AI Voice Agent for VeloTech

VeloTech sells fleet management software. The moment a lead submits a demo request, this agent calls them back, qualifies them through natural conversation, and logs everything for the sales team — no human touches the first call.

  • ✓ A structured qualification conversation covering intent, fit, and decision-making
  • ✓ Voicemail handling when no one picks up
Build steps
1

Create your outbound AI virtual agent

Set up the agent shell in the Aircall dashboard before touching any scripts.

  • In the Aircall dashboard, open AI Voice Agents
  • Click Build an AI Voice Agent and select Outbound
  • Give it a name that reflects VeloTech and the purpose of this agent
  • Choose its spoken language and its voice
2

Write the agent's opening message

The first five seconds decide whether the lead stays on the line.

  • Go to the Call script tab in the agent settings
  • Find the First message field
  • Write a short, natural greeting that identifies who is calling and why
  • Add variables like {{name}} by wrapping the variable name in double curly brackets
Tip 1 — the opening and farewell message are read exactly out loud, word for word. Write precisely what you want said, in the language you want it said in.
Tip 2 — imagine you just submitted a demo request form. What would you actually want to hear in the first five seconds?
3

Write the agent's behaviour prompt

This is the agent's brain: tone, goals, and every branch a real conversation can take.

  • In Call script → Conversation guidelines, describe the agent's tone and overall goal
  • Tell it what to do if the prospect starts asking questions back
  • Add the steps of qualification the agent must follow (e.g. "Step 1: ask …")
  • Define when the call is done — what a complete, successful conversation looks like
Tip — think about what a great first call from your best SDR looks like, then write the prompt so the agent behaves the same way. Use Markdown to keep it well structured.
# Conversation guidelines

## Identity & Transparency
- Always identify yourself as an AI assistant calling on behalf of Aircall at
  the start of the call
- Never claim or imply you are a human, even if asked directly
- If asked whether you are a bot or AI, confirm it clearly and without hesitation

## Greeting & Availability Check
- Greet the callee by their first name: "Hi {{first_name}}"
- Reference the form they submitted as the reason for the call — do not treat
  this as a cold call
- Confirm it is a good time before proceeding
- If it is NOT a good time: acknowledge it, offer a callback, and end
  gracefully — do not push forward
- If no response or unclear: ask once more, then offer a callback and close
  politely

## Purpose & Flow
- State the purpose in one sentence: to ask a couple of quick questions and
  connect them with the right person on the Aircall team
- Ask only one question at a time — never stack multiple questions in a
  single turn
- Keep the conversation tight — this is a qualification call, not a demo
  or pitch
- If the callee seems confused, remind them of the form they submitted —
  once, naturally

## Qualification Questions (ask in order, skip if already answered in the form)
- What does your team's current phone setup look like? (current stack)
- How many people on your team are regularly on calls with customers or
  prospects? (volume signal)
- What made you reach out to Aircall today — anything specific you are
  looking to solve? (intent signal)
- Are you the right person to evaluate this, or is someone else involved
  in the decision? (buyer check)

## Qualification Signals
- Strong fit: 5+ phone users, clear pain or intent stated, decision-maker
  on the line or accessible
- Weak fit: solo operator, no real call volume, vague intent, no path to
  a decision-maker
- Mixed signals: do not force it — offer to have a teammate reach out for
  a proper conversation

## Information Collection
- Only collect what is needed to route them correctly
- Confirm follow-up details before closing (best email, preferred
  callback time)
- Never invent or assume information the callee has not provided

## Next Steps & Handoff
- If qualified: confirm next steps clearly — "I'll pass your details to
  our team and someone will be in touch to set up a call. Does that work?"
- If not qualified: thank them genuinely and close politely — do not
  attempt to re-engage
- If they want to speak with someone now: initiate a transfer or confirm
  a teammate will call back promptly

## Escalation & Transfer
- If asked about pricing, features, integrations, or anything
  account-specific: do not attempt to answer — offer to connect them
  with a teammate
- If the callee requests a human at any point, initiate the transfer
  promptly
- If transfer is unavailable, confirm a teammate will follow up and
  specify how (call or email)

## Handling Objections & Disengagement
- If not interested: acknowledge politely and close — never push or
  repeat the pitch
- If they ask not to be contacted again: acknowledge immediately and end
  the call — do not attempt to re-engage
- If upset or frustrated: stay calm, offer a brief apology, offer a
  handoff or close politely — never argue

## Accuracy & Integrity
- Never speculate on pricing, product capabilities, or timelines
- If you do not have the answer, say so and offer to have the right
  person follow up
- Never make commitments on behalf of Aircall that were not explicitly
  scripted
4

Define the qualification questions

Structured intake questions the agent works through naturally during the call.

  • Go to the Intake questions section
  • Write 3–4 questions the agent will work through naturally during the call
  • Cover: company context, the problem they're solving, where they are in the buying process, and who else is involved
What is your company's name?
Establishes context — used to route and personalize the rest of the call.
What made you reach out?
Surfaces intent — the specific problem or trigger behind the demo request.
Any answers the virtual agent collects are saved as responses to these intake questions and logged both in the Aircall workspace and in any connected call-logging integrations.
5

Configure voicemail handling

Not every call connects — make the miss count too.

  • Find the Voicemail section in the Connect agent tab
  • Select Leave a message for "If voicemail is detected"
  • Write a short message to leave if no one picks up
  • Include who's calling and give them a reason to expect a follow-up
Hang up — end the call automatically when voicemail is detected
Leave a message — play a pre-recorded message before hanging up
"Hi, I'm reaching out from VeloTech. I haven't been able to reach you — if you'd like to reach us, you can call us at +49 12345678."
6

Test your agent

Ship it, call it, and listen back like a prospect would.

  • Save your configuration
  • Press the Test agent call button at the top right
Tip 1 — did the greeting feel natural? Did the questions flow? Did the voicemail sound human? Adjust and test again — building an agent is an iterative, continuous process.
Tip 2 — hit multiple issues while testing? Focus on one at a time. Reproduce it, fix it, then retest before moving to the next.
